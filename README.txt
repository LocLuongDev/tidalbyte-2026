Courseplan browser version. Extract the package and open index.html in Chrome or Edge.

The main workspace has Coursework and Calendar tabs in the content area, separate from the settings sidebar. Open Calendar to view monthly coursework deadlines and the matching study schedule calendar, or import a Canvas .ics file or subscription link. Each calendar has independent month navigation. The study schedule uses your selected courses and availability settings and plans up to the next 90 days. The last selected tab is remembered in the browser.

Visible courses includes Show all, Hide all, and Clear all data. Your Work Pace is configured in the settings sidebar. Each sidebar settings section can be expanded or collapsed independently, and the entire sidebar can be hidden.
